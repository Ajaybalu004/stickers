Sustainable clothing since 1981

At Switcher, we believe that the solution to more sustainable fashion lies not only in the use of ecological, sustainably produced, recycled fabrics, but also in the longevity of the final product.

Short distances

Everything in one place: from the yarn to the finished shirt, the entire production process takes place within a radius of 20 kilometers. This saves CO₂ and ensures full transparency.

Sustainable

We rely on responsibly grown raw materials and long-lasting quality. Every item is made to last - for you and the planet.

Fairly produced

Our textiles are produced under ethical conditions - with fair wages, safe workplaces and short transportation routes. This is how we reduce emissions right from the start.
